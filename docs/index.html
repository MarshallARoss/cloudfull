Cloudfull

Rediscover your photos and videos, and free up space, one item at a time.

What it does

  • Shows your photos and videos in a familiar format, so you can enjoy them again.
  • Play your videos on shuffle through your library.
  • Keep, share, shrink, or delete each memory as you go.
  • Shrinks large videos to save space, and shows you how much space you saved.
  • Keeps deleted items in an in-app Bin, so you can restore them before they are gone.
  • Keeps your photos and videos on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
