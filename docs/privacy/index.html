Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This policy explains what Cloudfull does with your data. Cloudfull is built to keep your photos and videos private, on your device.

Your photos and videos

Cloudfull shows the photos and videos already in your library. Cloudfull never uploads your photos or videos to us or to anyone else.

If you tap Share, iOS sends that item only to the app or person you choose. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs your library as usual, including changes you make in Cloudfull, such as a shrunk copy or an emptied Bin.

What Cloudfull remembers about your photos and videos, such as what you have seen, what you kept, and what is in your Bin, stays on your device. The anonymous usage record described below includes only totals, such as how many items are in your library.

Photo locations

Some photos carry a location where they were taken. To show a place name under a photo, Cloudfull sends that photo's embedded coordinate to Apple's Maps service. This lookup uses only the photo's own location, not your phone's current location. Apple's privacy policy applies to this lookup.

Anonymous usage analytics

Once each time you use Cloudfull, the app sends one anonymous record to Apple CloudKit, which is Cloudfull's own database hosted by Apple. The record uses a random install ID. This ID is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.

The record can include:

  • App version, iOS version, and device model
  • Number of items in your library and your free storage space
  • Session length
  • Country or region, language, and time zone
  • Network type
  • Display and accessibility settings, such as dark mode, text size, Reduce Motion, and Low Power Mode
  • Audio route and volume
  • Counts of feature use, such as keeps, deletes, shares, shrinks, tips, and Settings link taps

Cloudfull never sends your photos, videos, filenames, asset IDs, photo dates, photo locations, album names, or anything you type.

We use this data only to improve Cloudfull. For example, it shows us:

  • Which features people use, and which ones they do not find
  • Where the app is slow, such as how long the first video takes to load
  • Where people stop or change their mind, such as how often they restore items from the Bin
  • Whether a change helps, by comparing the totals before and after an update

We do not sell this data, and we do not share it with third parties. We do not use it for ads or for tracking, and Cloudfull does not use App Tracking Transparency or an IDFA.

Tips

Cloudfull offers optional tips through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. We never see your name or payment details. The anonymous usage record counts only which tip amount was chosen and whether the purchase finished. A tip unlocks nothing.

Data collected

In App Store terms, Cloudfull collects data not linked to your identity, for analytics only:

  • Usage Data: Product Interaction, Other Usage Data
  • Diagnostics: Performance Data
  • Identifiers: Device ID
  • Location: Coarse Location

Cloudfull collects nothing else, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Account and sign-in

Cloudfull has no account and no sign-up. There is nothing to register, and we hold no login information about you.

Deleting your data

Deleting Cloudfull from your device removes all of the app's on-device data, including your seen history, kept items, and Bin.

The anonymous usage records carry no name, email, or account. They use only a random ID that the app makes when you install it, and deleting the app removes that ID. Because of this, we usually cannot tell which records are yours. To ask about them, email fluffy@cloudfull.app and we'll respond with something like:

Hi, thanks for asking. Cloudfull's usage records hold only counts, such as how many items were kept or deleted. They have no name, email, or account, and they never include photos or videos. Each record carries only a random ID that the app makes when it is installed. The app does not show that ID, so we cannot find which records are yours. If you delete Cloudfull, that ID is gone, and no new record can connect to it. If you have more questions, reply to this email.

Children

Cloudfull is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
