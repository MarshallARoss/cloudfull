Privacy Policy

Effective date: RELEASE-DATE

This policy explains what Cloudfull does with your data. Cloudfull is built to keep your photos and videos private, on your device.

Your photos and videos

Cloudfull shows the photos and videos already in your library. Cloudfull does not upload your photos or videos to us or to anyone else.

If you tap Share, iOS sends that item only to the app or person you choose. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs your library as usual, including changes you make in Cloudfull, such as a shrunk copy or an emptied Bin.

What Cloudfull remembers about your photos and videos, such as what you have seen, what you kept, and what is in your Bin, stays on your device. The anonymous usage record described below includes only totals, such as how many items are in your library.

Photo locations

Some photos carry a location where they were taken. To show a place name under a photo, Cloudfull sends that photo's embedded coordinate to Apple's Maps service. This lookup uses only the photo's own location, not your phone's current location. Apple's privacy policy applies to this lookup.

Anonymous usage analytics

Once each time you use Cloudfull, the app sends one anonymous record to Apple CloudKit, which is Cloudfull's own database hosted by Apple. The record uses a random install ID. This ID is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.

The record can include:

  • App version, iOS version, and device model
  • Number of items in your library and your free storage space
  • Session length
  • Country or region, language, and time zone
  • Network type
  • Display and accessibility settings, such as dark mode, text size, Reduce Motion, and Low Power Mode
  • Audio route and volume
  • Counts of feature use, such as keeps, deletes, shares, shrinks, tips, purchases, and Settings link taps
  • Whether an Apple Ads ad brought the install, and details of that ad, such as its campaign, ad group, and keyword numbers

Cloudfull does not send your photos, videos, filenames, asset IDs, photo dates, photo locations, album names, or anything you type.

We use this data to improve Cloudfull, and to see which Apple Ads campaign brought an install. For example, it shows us:

  • Which features people use, and which ones they do not find
  • Where the app is slow, such as how long the first video takes to load
  • Where people stop or change their mind, such as how often they restore items from the Bin
  • Whether a change helps, by comparing the totals before and after an update

Purchases

Cloudfull offers a one-time Lifetime unlock and a monthly subscription, both through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes every payment. We do not see your name, your Apple ID, or your payment details.

The app reads a purchase record that Apple signs and stores on your device, to know whether your unlock is active. The anonymous usage record notes which plan is active (none, trial, monthly, lifetime, or an early buyer), how often the purchase screen was shown and closed, which plan a purchase attempt was for, how it ended and which locked action led to it, how many days the monthly plan has been active and whether it is set to renew, and whether Restore Purchases or Manage Subscription was used. It notes the plan's price tier, for example 299. The usage record does not include the amount you paid, the currency, the transaction id, an offer code, your name, or your Apple ID.

Cloudfull also offers optional tips through the same In-App Purchase system. A tip unlocks nothing.

Ad measurement

The App Store version of Cloudfull has code from Meta, Google, and TikTok, and uses it to measure Cloudfull's own ads. This code sends these companies your app activity, such as when you install or open Cloudfull, view a screen, see the purchase screen, or buy a plan, with purchase details such as the price and the currency (Google also gets the App Store transaction ID). It also sends a device ID, device and network details, diagnostic data, and your IP address. Google's code also learns whether an Apple Ads ad or a Google ad brought the install. Meta and TikTok promise in their terms to protect this data against unauthorized access and disclosure. Google promises in its Analytics terms not to share it with others, except in limited cases. Meta and TikTok also use the data under their own privacy policies, for example to improve their services and to personalize ads. Google does not use it for its own ad targeting. Cloudfull uses Google Analytics. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Meta and TikTok may use the information their code collects from Cloudfull to measure ads and to target and deliver ads. If you have a Meta or TikTok account, you can choose whether they use your activity in other apps for the ads they show you: Meta: Activity from other businesses, TikTok: Targeted ads. Meta says apps may still send it your activity.

Account and sign-in

Cloudfull has no account and no sign-up. There is nothing to register, and we hold no login information about you.

Deleting your data

Deleting Cloudfull from your device removes all of the app's on-device data, including your seen history, kept items, and Bin.

We keep the anonymous usage records until we delete them. There is no fixed period.

The anonymous usage records carry no name, email, or account. They use only a random ID that the app makes when you install it, and deleting the app removes that ID. Because of this, we usually cannot tell which records are yours. To ask about them email us and we'll respond with something like:

Hi, thanks for asking. Cloudfull's usage records hold only counts, such as how many items were kept or deleted. They have no name, email, or account, and they do not include photos or videos. Each record carries only a random ID that the app makes when it is installed. The app does not show that ID, so we cannot find which records are yours. If you delete Cloudfull, that ID is gone, and no new record can connect to it. If you have more questions, reply to this email.

Deleting Cloudfull stops all data collection by the app. It does not delete what Meta, Google, or TikTok already received; they keep it as their privacy policies say. To ask them about it, use their privacy policies linked under Ad measurement.

Children
