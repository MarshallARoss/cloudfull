Free to download

Cloudfull is free to download from the App Store. You can scroll through your photos and videos at no cost.

Subscription

Cloudfull offers a monthly subscription at $2.99 per month, or the local equivalent shown in the App Store. It renews automatically each month unless you cancel first.

To cancel, open iOS Settings, go to your Apple ID, then Subscriptions, and cancel Cloudfull there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged. Apple, not Cloudfull, charges your Apple ID for the subscription.

Lifetime purchase

Cloudfull also offers a one-time Lifetime purchase for $9.99, or the local equivalent shown in the App Store. This is a single payment, not a subscription, and it unlocks the app for good.

If you bought Cloudfull when it was a paid download (version 1.0), you keep everything. No purchase is needed. If the app ever asks you to buy, tap Restore Purchases in Settings.

If you buy the Lifetime unlock while a monthly subscription is still running, the subscription does not cancel on its own. Cancel it yourself in iOS Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions, so it does not renew again.
