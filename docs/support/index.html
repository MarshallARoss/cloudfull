FAQ

Why does Cloudfull need Photos access?

Cloudfull shows the photos and videos already in your library. It needs full access to read and manage your library, and to save items you keep, shrink, or delete.

Where do deleted items go?

Deleted items first go to the Bin inside Cloudfull, so you can review or restore them. When you empty the Bin, the items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. They stay there for 30 days before Photos removes them for good.

Why is there a Bin? Why can't Cloudfull delete items right away?

iOS asks you to confirm every time an app deletes photos or videos. Without the Bin, you would see that dialog after every single delete. With the Bin, you delete as many items as you like, then confirm once for the whole batch.

What does Shrink do?

Shrink re-encodes a video to reduce its size and get you space. The new copy keeps the original's date and location. Cloudfull shows the size before and after, and how much space you saved. The original video then goes to the Bin.

What format does Shrink convert from and to?

Shrink converts a 4K (or larger) video into a smaller 1080p HEVC video, saved as a QuickTime .mov file.

  • Which videos qualify: 4K and up
  • Output: HEVC (H.265), 1920×1080, .mov. Same frame rate and aspect ratio as the original.
  • HDR: an HDR original (HLG, wide color) stays HDR in the copy, with the same color tags.
  • Audio: kept as is — same codec, channels, and sample rate as the original.
  • Typical bitrate: the new video track measured 6–13 Mbps in our tests, well below a typical 4K original.
  • Metadata: the copy carries the original's date and location.
  • The 90% rule: Cloudfull only swaps in the copy if it is smaller than 90% of the original size. Otherwise Cloudfull says the video "is already efficiently compressed" and leaves the original untouched.
  • Safety order: the original moves to the Bin only after the new copy finishes saving. Nothing is permanently deleted until you empty the Bin, and Recently Deleted in Photos clears it 30 days after that.

What is the "Cloudfull keeps" album?

When you keep an item, Cloudfull saves it into a "Cloudfull keeps" album in your Photos app.

How do I turn on the daily reminder?

Open Cloudfull, go to Settings, and turn on Daily “On this date”. Then pick the time you want the reminder.

Does Cloudfull need an account?

No. Cloudfull has no account and no sign-up. It runs on your iOS device with your media. You can start using it right away.
