How to fix a full iPhone

If you're seeing 'iCloud storage full' try out these steps before paying more

  1. See what fills the phone. Open Settings > General > iPhone Storage.
  2. Look at Photos first. Videos usually take the most space.
  3. Dedupe. In Photos, open Utilities > Duplicates and merge the copies.
  4. Scroll the rest with Cloudfull. Keep, shrink, or bin each photo and video.
  5. Clear Recently Deleted.
