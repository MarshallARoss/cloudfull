Cloudfull on iPhone Duo

Your camera roll, one video at a time. Layouts for the Duo’s poses, on day one.

Every pose

Closed

The controls sit in the system column, next to the status ring.

Closed and turned sideways

A wide video fills the whole outer screen.

Open in a wide layout

Tall videos fit the full height. Wide videos fit with every control visible.

Partially folded, like a book

The video plays on the left page. The right page shows the other moments from the same day, or the same week.

Open in a tall layout

The closed layout, bigger. Tall videos are never cropped.

Folded like a laptop

Video on top. Keep, Share, Shrink, Delete and Previous / Next on the bottom half.

How it is built

  • Real interface rotation, not a fixed layout.
  • Size classes, not device checks.
  • Reserved regions for the fold and the camera.
  • Hinge status, so the layout knows when the phone is folding.
  • No UIScreen.main anywhere in the code.
  • The layout on a regular iPhone is unchanged.
  • Built and tested in Xcode 27.1 Device Hub.

Cloudfull follows Apple's iPhone Duo guidance.

Private by design

Cloudfull never uploads your photos or videos. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking. Your photos and videos stay on your phone, in every iPhone Duo pose.
